About

I am a quantitative researcher working on systematic investing, and a Ph.D. candidate in finance at Hanyang University, advised by Prof. Hyoung-Goo Kang. My academic work is in empirical asset pricing: how investor disagreement is priced when the usual measures are missing, using the dispersion of machine-learning return forecasts as the measure.

In industry I build the path from research to production: portfolio construction, out-of-sample evaluation of alphas, and the quant logic inside LLM-driven research systems.
